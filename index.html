A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What if I’m not sure which platform I need?+

Tell me what you want the site to do and how your team will use it. We can choose WordPress, Shopify, or Webflow around those needs.

Can you work with my existing design or website?+

Yes. I can translate a Figma design into a live website, improve an existing storefront, or rebuild a site that has become difficult to manage.

How much will my project cost?+

A 1–5 page website is $200 USD. The 6–10 page package is $500 USD and includes articles and on-page SEO. The 10+ page package starts at $800 USD and includes 5 free revisions, technical SEO, and on-page SEO. For a 10-page site, choose the package that matches the support you need. We’ll confirm the scope and final price before starting.

What happens after launch?+

The process includes a handoff so you can manage your content. We can also discuss post-launch fixes and ongoing support based on your needs.
